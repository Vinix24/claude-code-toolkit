# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

First version. Replaces the private `debunk` and `maker` skills with one skill and a different tone: it helps, it does not unmask.

- Handles comment gates and search requests.
- Checks first whether the promised thing already exists: in the post, from the maker, or as a product.
- Chooses the form before building: just buy it, paste prompt, skill, plugin, proof of concept or site.
- Every answer has the same eight sections, ending with a receipt to tick off after the build.
- Never copies the post; conditions are written in its own words.
- `PLAK-PROMPT.md` with the same steps for ChatGPT and Claude in the browser.
